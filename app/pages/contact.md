## How to reach us

- **Email:** [musicamalonllc@gmail.com](mailto:musicamalonllc@gmail.com).
- **Corrections and new claims:** use the [Suggest a person or feature](/feedback) form, or the "Suggest a correction or a claim" link on any person's page. Please include at least two trusted, independent sources (see [How we score](/methodology)).

## What to write to us about

- **A wrong number.** Tell us which person, which entry, and which source you think disagrees with it. If two trusted sources back a lower number, we use the lower one.
- **A missing claim.** Send the claim and two independent, very trusted sources that agree on the figure. Claims without that support are listed as "unproven" and don't affect the score.
- **Requests from people listed on the site, or from their representatives.** We review these by hand. Please tell us which entry you are asking about and why.
- **Privacy questions.** See our [Privacy policy](/privacy), or email us.
- **Business, press or advertising.** Email us.

We read every message. We usually reply within a few business days, but we can't promise a reply to every one.
