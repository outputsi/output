## Who actually changed the world, by the numbers?

**Output ranks famous people by the measurable impact they had on Earth, both good and bad.** We don't use opinion polls, follower counts or net worth. We count concrete results in real units: lives saved or lost, people fed, jobs created, money in fines and penalties, and major awards.

Every counted number has to be confirmed by **at least two independent, very trusted sources**. That means peer-reviewed journals, government records, official regulatory filings, national archives, major museums and newspapers of record. When sources disagree, we use the **lowest** documented figure. Harms count only if a court or official body found them. Claims that don't meet these rules are still shown on the person's page, marked **unproven**, but they never affect the score.

Units are never added together. A life is never turned into dollars, and jobs aren't turned into awards. Each measure is scored on its own on a log scale, and the **Overall** tab adds those signed scores, with lives weighted most. Harms always pull a score down.

That's why a wheat scientist can outrank billionaires.
