# Warren Buffett: summary
*Source page: https://outputrank.com/people/warren-buffett. Overall rank #6, score +87*

Warren Buffett ranks #6 overall, with a score of **+87**. He is the highest-ranked person on the list who has both a counted positive entry and a counted negative one. That makes his page a clear example of how Output nets good and bad. [E1, E2]

**Jobs: +392,400 (score +88).** Berkshire Hathaway's 2024 Form 10-K, filed with the SEC and also published on Berkshire's site, says that "Berkshire and its operating subsidiaries employed approximately 392,400 people worldwide at the end of 2024." The credit type is *Organization*: Buffett led the company as CEO and chairman. NPR reported that he stepped down as CEO at the start of 2026, at age 95, and remains chairman. [E1]

**Economy: −$896,000 (score −2).** In 2014 Berkshire Hathaway agreed to pay a **civil penalty of $896,000** to settle a US Justice Department and FTC case. The case was about a missed Hart-Scott-Rodino premerger filing tied to its USG stake, and it ended in a consent Final Judgment. Reuters quotes Buffett: "We made a mistake when we overlooked the filing requirement." Because it is an official finding, it counts as a harm. [E2]

**Why the fine barely moves the score.** Economy is scored in millions of dollars and weighted only 0.25×, because fines are penalties, not direct harm to people. A sub-$1 million fine therefore costs Buffett only 2 points. Under Output's rules, it can only lower his score, never raise it.

**What isn't counted.** A 2020 OFAC settlement of $4,144,651 is listed as **unproven**. It covered 144 apparent violations of Iran sanctions by Iscar's Turkish subsidiary. It is shown for transparency but doesn't count. [U1]

**Reach.** Buffett has Wikipedia articles in 104 languages and had 2.29 million English Wikipedia page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. This is not part of the score.
