# Fritz Haber: summary
*Source page: https://outputrank.com/people/fritz-haber. Overall rank #4, score +100 (est.)*

Fritz Haber is ranked #4 overall and holds the top score on Output's **People** measure: **+1.2 billion people (est.)**, a People score of **+100**. [E1]

**What is being counted.** Haber co-developed the Haber-Bosch process, which makes ammonia for nitrogen fertilizer. Two peer-reviewed sources estimate how many people depend on it for food:
- Vaclav Smil (1999) wrote that "about 40% of today's 6 billion people are alive thanks to the Haber-Bosch synthesis of ammonia," or about 2.4 billion people.
- Erisman et al. (2008, *Nature Geoscience*) found that "by 2000, nitrogen fertilizers were responsible for feeding 44% of the world's population."

Output takes the **lower figure, 2.4 billion**. It then **splits the credit evenly with Carl Bosch**, which leaves Haber with 1.2 billion. Because both sources give approximate shares, the entry is labeled **est.** [E1]

The page counts this under *people*, not *lives*. That is why Haber's +100 comes from the People measure, with weight 1×, while Borlaug's billion is counted as Lives, with weight 3×.

**What isn't counted.**
- **Positive, unproven:** a larger estimate that Haber-Bosch fertilizer has supported about 4 billion people born since 1908 (Erisman et al.; Our World in Data). It isn't counted. [U1]
- **Negative, unproven:** Haber proposed using chlorine gas, and a claim says the German chlorine attack at Ypres on 22 April 1915 killed 5,000 French and Algerian troops. The sources **conflict**. Britannica's figure is challenged by a Springer source that says the attack "reportedly injured about 5000 and killed about 1000." Because the death toll isn't agreed, it doesn't count yet. Output also requires a court or official finding for harms. [U2]

The chlorine-gas harm is disputed among sources (about 1,000 vs 5,000 deaths) and is not yet scored.

So Haber's current score shows only his counted positive entry. The unproven chemical-weapons claim is shown on his page for transparency.

**Reach.** Haber has Wikipedia articles in 88 languages and had 335,155 English Wikipedia page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. This is not part of the score.
