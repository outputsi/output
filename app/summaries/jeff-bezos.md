# Jeff Bezos: summary
*Source page: https://outputrank.com/people/jeff-bezos. Overall rank #7, score +86*

Jeff Bezos ranks #7 overall, with **+86**. That comes from a large jobs score (**Jobs +97**) minus two counted European fines (**Economy −11**). His page also has one of the longest lists of unproven claims on the site, which shows how strict Output's rules are. [E1–E3]

**Jobs: about 1.3 million.** Amazon's Form 10-K for 2020, filed with the SEC, says: "As of December 31, 2020, we employed approximately 1,298,000 full-time and part-time employees." Fortune reported the count had grown to more than 1.3 million by mid-2021. The credit type is *Organization*, because Bezos founded and ran Amazon. [E1]

**Counted fines (negative):**
- **€35 million, France, 2020.** The French data-protection authority, CNIL, fined Amazon Europe Core for placing advertising cookies on amazon.fr users' devices without prior consent. France's Conseil d'État upheld the fine in June 2022. Bezos was CEO at the time. [E2]
- **€4 million, France, 2019.** The Paris Commercial Court fined two Amazon companies over "significantly unbalanced" contract clauses with marketplace sellers, after a government investigation. The page notes it was a first-instance ruling and that no appellate reversal was found. [E3]

Each fine is kept in its own currency-year unit, and economy is weighted only 0.25×, so together they cost him 11 points.

**Not counted: positive.** These are all shown as unproven:
- The $10 billion Bezos Earth Fund. It is a pledge, not a measured effect.
- The Day 1 Families Fund's report of helping over 32,000 families. The second source gives no family count.
- $33M for 1,000 DACA scholarships. One source is the recipient, which is not independent.
- 200 Bezos Academy preschool seats. [U1–U4]

**Not counted: negative.** These are shown as unproven:
- The 2021 FTC Flex-tips case: 141,128 drivers repaid.
- The 2025 FTC Prime settlement: refunds to an estimated 35 million customers, with no admission of wrongdoing.
- A €746 million Luxembourg GDPR fine, which a court annulled.
- A €1.128 billion Italian fine. A court said it had to be recalculated, with no revised figure given. [U5–U8]

**Reach.** Bezos has Wikipedia articles in 101 languages and had 10.6 million English Wikipedia page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. This is not part of the score.
