# Mary Barra: summary
*Source page: https://outputrank.com/people/mary-barra. Overall rank #10, score +82*

Mary Barra, chair and CEO of General Motors, rounds out Output's top 10 with **+82**. All of it comes from jobs: **156,000**. [E1]

**Where the number comes from.** GM's Form 10-K for 2025, filed with the US SEC, breaks the workforce down at 31 December 2025: "approximately 88,000 (56%) hourly employees and approximately 68,000 (44%) salaried employees." Those two figures add to 156,000. The same filing's table is in thousands and shows "Total Worldwide 155." Output counts 156,000, the sum of the hourly and salaried figures stated in the filing. [E1]

**Why she gets the credit.** The same filing lists "Mary T. Barra (64) | Chair and Chief Executive Officer (2016)." Credit type is *Organization*: GM's workforce is credited to its leader. [E1]

**How the score works.** Jobs is scored on a log scale against the largest jobs figure on the site, Walmart's 2.1 million. On that scale, 156,000 jobs gives +82, only a few points behind companies with larger workforces, such as Microsoft and LVMH. Every person in places 6 to 10 is scored mainly on company headcount from official filings. That is a reminder that Output measures *documented* impact, and corporate headcount is among the best-documented numbers there are.

**What isn't counted.** One negative claim is listed as **unproven**. Under a September 2015 deferred prosecution agreement in New York (SDNY), GM agreed to forfeit **$900 million** for concealing an ignition-switch defect. The page lists it with two US Justice Department sources but marks it unproven, so it has no effect on her score. It is shown for transparency. [U1]

**Reach.** Barra has Wikipedia articles in 37 languages and had 201,778 English Wikipedia page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. This is not part of the score.
