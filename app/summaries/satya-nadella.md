# Satya Nadella: summary
*Source page: https://outputrank.com/people/satya-nadella. Overall rank #8, score +85*

Satya Nadella, chairman and CEO of Microsoft, ranks #8 overall with a score of **+85**. All of it comes from one counted entry: **about 228,000 jobs**. [E1]

**Where the number comes from.** Microsoft's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2025, filed with the US SEC, says: "As of June 30, 2025, we employed approximately 228,000 people on a full-time basis, 125,000 in the U.S. and 103,000 internationally." Under Output's rules, a legally required filing like this is enough on its own for a company's headcount. [E1]

**Why he gets the credit.** Output credits an organization's results to the person leading it (credit type *Organization*). A second SEC filing on the entry confirms that "Mr. Nadella was appointed Chairman of the Board in June 2021 and Chief Executive Officer in February 2014." [E1]

**What the figure includes, and what it leaves out.** The 228,000 figure counts full-time employees only, as the filing reports them. Output doesn't add contractors, suppliers or estimated indirect jobs. Those would need their own claim with two trusted sources, and none has been submitted.

**How the score works.** Jobs is scored on a log scale against the largest jobs figure on the site, Walmart's 2.1 million under Doug McMillon. On a log scale, 228,000 is still most of the way there, so Nadella scores +85, close to leaders whose companies employ many more people. Jobs carries a weight of 1×.

**What isn't counted.** The page lists no negative entries and no unproven claims, positive or negative. That doesn't mean Microsoft has no record. It means no claim has yet met, or been submitted under, Output's sourcing rules. Anyone can suggest one with sources.

**Reach.** Nadella has Wikipedia articles in 56 languages and had 748,200 English Wikipedia page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. This is not part of the score.
