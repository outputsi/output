Bernard Arnault, chairman and CEO of LVMH, ranks #9 overall with **+84**. All of it comes from jobs: **211,552 employees**. [E1]

**Where the number comes from.** LVMH's 2025 Universal Registration Document is the French equivalent of an annual regulatory filing. It was filed with France's market regulator, the AMF, on 31 March 2026, under reference D.26-0195. It states: "Excluding apprenticeships and vocational training, total headcount stood at 211,552 employees, a decrease of 2% compared with the 215,637 total headcount published at December 31, 2024." A filing that the law requires counts on its own for headcount, and a GlobeNewswire notice confirms the AMF filing reference. [E1]

**Why he gets the credit.** The same document states that "Bernard Arnault has been Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the Company since 1989." So the credit type is *Organization*: the group's workforce is credited to the person who has led it for more than three decades. [E1]

**The page uses the latest, lower number.** LVMH's headcount fell about 2% in 2025, from 215,637 to 211,552. The page counts the newer, lower figure. That matches Output's conservative approach of using the lowest documented number.

**What isn't counted.** One negative claim is listed as **unproven**. The French Justice Ministry's own text notes that validating a CJIP "does not amount to a declaration of guilt." The page marks it unproven, so it doesn't count toward his score. It is shown for transparency. [U1]
