# Mark Zuckerberg: summary
*Source page: https://outputrank.com/people/mark-zuckerberg. Overall rank #20, score +57*

Mark Zuckerberg ranks #20 overall, with a score of **+57**. The page shows Economy **−20** and Jobs **+77**. Counted European fines lower that jobs score. Two much larger penalty claims are on the page as unproven and are not scored. [E1–E3, U1, U2]

**Jobs: 74,067 (score +77).** Meta reported headcount of **74,067** at 31 December 2024, up 10% from a year earlier. The leadership page on the entry says Zuckerberg is the founder, chairman, and CEO of Meta, which he founded as Facebook in 2004. Credit is *Organization*. [E1]

**Counted fines (score −20).** The page's economy total is about **$1.09 billion** in losses.
- On 14 November 2024 the European Commission fined Meta **€797.72 million** for tying Facebook Marketplace to Facebook and imposing unfair trading conditions. The BBC report on the same entry says Meta was fined €798 million, and that Meta rejected the findings and would appeal. The counted scale is the Commission's €797.72 million. The page says EU appeals have no suspensive effect and that no stay or reversal was found, so the fine is scored. [E2]
- On 23 April 2025 the Commission fined Meta **€200 million** over its "consent or pay" model for March to November 2024. The page counts that fine as well. [E3]

**Not scored.** The notable harms below are listed as unproven. They do not change the +57.
- Irish Data Protection Commission GDPR fines are grouped in one unproven entry with scale 0: **€1.2 billion** in 2023 (the DPC quote shows EUR 1,200,000,000), **€405 million**, **€390 million**, and **€265 million** in 2022, and **€251 million** and **€91 million** in 2024. The DPC quote on the entry says the €1.2 billion decision is pending appeal, and that a fine cannot be collected while an appeal is pending. These fines are not scored. [U1]
- A 2019 FTC press release on the entry announces a **$5 billion** civil penalty and new privacy restrictions on Facebook. That claim is unproven and is not scored. [U2]

**Reach.** Zuckerberg has Wikipedia articles in 143 languages and had 2.25 million English Wikipedia page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. This is not part of the score.
