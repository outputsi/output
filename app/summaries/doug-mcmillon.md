# Doug McMillon: summary
*Source page: https://outputrank.com/people/doug-mcmillon. Overall rank #3, score +100*

Doug McMillon, the long-time CEO of Walmart, ranks #3 overall. His score comes entirely from jobs. Output credits him with **about 2.1 million jobs**, the largest jobs figure on the site. That gives him the maximum Jobs score of **+100**. [E1]

**Where the number comes from.** Walmart's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended 31 January 2026, filed with the US SEC, says the company serves customers through "approximately 2.1 million associates around the world": about 1.6 million in the US and about 0.5 million internationally. A legally required filing like this is enough on its own for a company's headcount under Output's rules. [E1]

**Why he gets the credit.** Output credits an organization's results to the person who led it (credit type *Organization*). Two Walmart sources on the page confirm his role:
- A 2013 Walmart filing announced that the board elected "company veteran Doug McMillon, 47" to succeed Mike Duke as president and CEO, effective 1 February 2014.
- A November 2025 Walmart announcement says John Furner will succeed him as CEO from 1 February 2026, and that McMillon would retire on 31 January 2026. [E1]

So the 2.1 million figure is the workforce of the company he ran for 12 years, measured at the end of his last fiscal year as CEO.

**Why one entry is enough for #3.** Each measure is scored against the largest amount anyone has on it. Walmart's headcount is the largest on the Jobs ranking, so McMillon sets the scale at +100. Other leaders' jobs scores are measured against his.

**What isn't counted.** The page lists no negative entries and no unproven claims, positive or negative. McMillon's score is only as complete as the claims submitted so far. Anyone can suggest a sourced claim.

**Reach.** McMillon has Wikipedia articles in 11 languages and had 119,784 English Wikipedia page views from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. This is not part of the score.
